Lumbre Studios/Chance Darts/Training

Five training games

GIVE PRACTICE
A PURPOSE.

Find your doubles. Work around the board. Add a little pressure. Choose a training game that gives your next session a clear focus.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. Optional lifetime Pro.

Checkout Training

Turn finishing into a focused exercise. Choose a fixed target or random band, 2–6 darts per attempt and 1–50 attempts per player. Targets must be finishable with the selected dart count.

Work on the finish

Checkout Training uses a double finish. The remaining darts and attempt state keep longer attempts clear across visits. Supports 1–16 players.

Round the Board

Move through targets 1–20, with optional bull targets. Choose Any Segment, Doubles Only or Trebles Only to focus your accuracy.

Build consistency

Enter each dart on the actual bed hit and follow the advancing target. Trebles Only excludes bull. Supports 1–16 players.

Half-It

Play the selected preset’s sequence of targets. Qualifying hits add points; a visit with no qualifying score halves your accumulated total, rounded down.

Make the target count

Number, bull and segment challenges reward hitting what the round asks for. The changing targets bring pressure to a practice session. Supports 1–16 players.

Cricket

Close 20 through 15 and bull with three marks each. Extra marks score while at least one opponent still has the target open.

Accuracy meets tactics

Close every target and have at least as many points as every opponent to win. Standard scoring Cricket supports 2–12 players.

Killer

Each player receives a different number. Build activation marks on your own number, then remove opponents’ lives with hits on theirs.

A competitive practice session

Choose 1–5 starting lives, exact activation, double-to-activate and self-hit penalty options. The last surviving player wins. Supports 2–16 players.

Training results reflect committed darts. Play Again returns to setup. There is no general Training history browser or mid-session restore screen.
