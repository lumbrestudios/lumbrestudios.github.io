Online Play & live video

TWO BOARDS.
ONE MATCH.

Share the competition, wherever your boards are. Real-time scoring brings two players together, with live camera sharing when you choose.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. Optional lifetime Pro.

GET CONNECTED.

  1. Create the match

    Sign in with your public display name. The Pro host chooses 301, 501 or 701, best of 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9 legs, and Double In / Double Out.

  2. Invite your opponent

    Share the 10-character Online Match code privately. Your opponent chooses Join Online Match on another supported device and signs in. Joining is free.

  3. Ready. Then play.

    Both players mark themselves ready. The host starts the match, and scoring follows the active player and confirmed match state.

A view of the other board.

Position your device securely with the dartboard in frame and the scorer within reach. Use a clear, well-lit view and keep private surroundings out of shot. Each player chooses whether to share or receive video.

You choose the camera

Camera sharing is optional and uses no microphone. The app does not provide video recording. Continue entering your darts or visit totals in the scorer.

Keep control

Stop sharing when you need to. Report or block is available from Exit Match, and Online safety lets you manage blocked accounts. Opening safety controls stops video.

Stay with the match.

If the connection drops, use Retry or Reconnect when offered and wait for the confirmed scores before entering another visit. Real-time play and video depend on both devices’ internet connections and network conditions.

Confirmed scores matter

Check the shared state before repeating an unconfirmed submission. At the end of a leg, the host starts the next one.

Play another

Completed matches offer rematch controls for a new session. Confirming Exit during an active match ends it for both players; shared save and resume is not available.
