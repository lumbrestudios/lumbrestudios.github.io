EVERY VISIT.EVERY DOUBLE.YOUR PROGRESS.CHANCE DARTS / LUMBRE STUDIOS

Your player. Your record.

Save a player profile and select it for the match to link supported records to that player. Explore player performance, career history and the progression that the app has recorded.

Performance in context

Three-dart average, first-nine average, checkout percentage, doubles, high scores and wins tell different parts of the story. Check recorded attempts before comparing percentages.

Progress over time

Levels, XP, achievements and personal bests bring context to the matches you complete. Detailed progression and advanced performance views require Pro.

See where your darts land.

Individual dart entry records segment hits for supported heatmaps. Explore total tracked darts, most-hit categories and the corresponding text list without relying on colour alone.

Input sets the detail

A total-only visit records the score but cannot describe which exact segments you hit. A heatmap shows recorded dart activity, not inferred throws.

Free and Pro

Local profiles and match results provide the starting point. Advanced Pro statistics require Pro and a supported saved profile; guest profiles do not receive Pro statistics.

Keep your history in context.

Saved Games is for resumable local matches. Completed results, team history, league fixtures and Darts Night history live in their own areas.

On this device

Local profiles and local history stay with the app on that device. Signing into a Chance Darts account is not a general cloud backup.

Training results

Review the current training session at completion. Do not assume the existence of a general Training history browser or mid-session restore.
