Getting started

What can I play offline?

Local scoring, Training, local profiles and saved local records are available on the device, subject to applicable Pro checks. Local Duo Live Display also works offline. Online Play, Shared League updates, browser / TV Live Display and store purchases need their services to be available.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

The local free-play allowance is three completed matches. One free League Organiser starter, up to four entrants, is shared across Local and Shared Leagues. Joining Online Play and participating in authorised Shared League fixtures remain free. Pro unlocks unlimited local play, Online hosting, more organiser access and advanced performance features.

Scoring

How do I enter a score?

Use Single, Double or Treble and the number hit, with Bull and Miss where offered. Enter commits the visit. Turn and quick totals enter a whole visit. Answer finishing-dart or double prompts when the scorer requests them.

Can I correct a mistake or resume later?

Undo Dart and Undo Turn correct eligible input. Save supported local matches to Saved Games for later resuming. Online and fixture-managed sessions have their own restrictions. Training has no mid-session restore screen.

How do Pots work?

180 adds 50 Pot points, 140 adds 40, 120 adds 30 and 100 adds 20. A visit of 26 deducts 10 from each opposing-side player. The configured match length ends play; the highest Pot wins, with partners combined in Doubles.

Online Play

How do we start an online match?

Both players sign in with public display names. The Pro host creates a match and shares its 10-character Online Match code. The other player joins free. Both mark ready in the waiting room, then the host starts.

Does video enter scores for me?

No. Optional camera sharing lets you show your board. Enter the darts or visit total yourself. Video uses no microphone, and the app does not provide recording. Position the device securely and keep private information out of frame.

What if the connection drops?

Use Retry or Reconnect when offered. Wait for confirmed scores and check whether your last submission was accepted before entering it again. Network conditions can affect scoring and video. Confirming Exit ends an active match for both players.

How do I report or block someone?

Use Exit Match, then Report or block. Opening those controls stops video. Online Play → Online safety also lets you report recent opponents and manage blocks. A report is confirmed only after the service accepts it.

Live Display

Which code do I use?

Online Match codes are 10 characters and join an opponent. Live Display uses eight letters or digits, usually grouped four and four, and connects a spectator. Use the display code at the browser receiver or in the Apple TV app. The codes are not interchangeable.

How do I connect a browser or Apple TV?

Start Live Display from a supported Match Play or Darts Night session. Open chancedarts.lumbrestudios.app on a compatible browser, or the native TV receiver. Enter the display code. Continue scoring on the original device.

Why is my screen showing old scores?

A receiver can retain the last scores while reconnecting. Check the scorer is still broadcasting and both devices are connected. Confirm the current display code. If the scorer ended its display, start a new display session rather than repeatedly using the old code.

iPhone Duo

Why does Duo Live Display ask for camera access?

The system needs an active camera capture session to enable simultaneous inner and outer display operation. Duo’s session does not analyse, record or transmit frames and uses no microphone. The camera indicator is expected while enabled.

Can Duo display and online video run together?

They need the same camera resource. Stop Duo Live Display before starting Online Video or another camera feature. Local Duo can run alongside remote browser and TV displays in supported Match Play and Darts Night.

What happens after an interruption?

Backgrounding or a camera interruption stops local Duo display. Re-enable it when ready. Brief screen dimming may occur when moving the device or adjusting the hinge; the accepted implementation retains this limitation.

Apple Watch

What does the Watch companion support?

Live viewing of supported two-player local singles matches: scores, current-player focus, match progress and completed results. It reconnects when reopened. Input stays on iPhone. Online, training, Pots, doubles and fixture-managed sessions are outside this scope.

Competitions

How many people can join Darts Night?

A Darts Night is a local round robin for 3–6 individual players, using saved profiles or guests. Everyone plays everyone once. Six players means 15 fixtures. For teams and longer competitions, use Leagues.

Why has a shared result not changed the table?

Submission and confirmation are separate. A pending or disputed result is not a confirmed standings result. The opposing captain can confirm or dispute where permitted; organiser override is separate. Refresh after a connection error before resubmitting.

Progress & purchases

Why is my heatmap missing some darts?

Individual dart entry records the segments hit. A total-only visit cannot supply those segments. Heatmaps describe tracked darts only. Advanced statistics also require Pro and a supported saved player profile; guest profiles do not receive Pro statistics.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Pro and choose Restore Purchases with the platform store account used to buy it. App-account sign-in is separate. Check the store connection and the result shown before attempting another purchase. A purchase on one store is not a promise of ownership on another.

Does my account back up all my history?

No. Local profiles and histories stay with the app on this device. Shared Leagues and Online Play use connected services. Do not uninstall or clear app data to resolve missing records; check the relevant player or competition and contact support.

Support

How do I get help?

Email support@lumbrestudios.app with your app version, device, screen and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details or active connection codes. Support & About in the app also links to support and privacy information.

Need a hand?

Email support@lumbrestudios.app. Include your app version, device and the steps that led to the issue.
