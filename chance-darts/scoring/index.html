Match Play

THE SCORE.
CENTRE STAGE.

From a quick 301 to a match decided in sets. Build your game, choose your scoring style and keep every visit in view.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. Optional lifetime Pro.

Make it your match.

Start at 301, 501 or 701. Choose Singles or Doubles, set the throw order and switch Double In and Double Out to suit your game. Link saved player profiles or use the names for this match.

Legs or sets

Without sets, Best of Legs offers 1–10. With sets, choose 1, 3, 5 or 7 legs per set and best of 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9 sets.

Singles, doubles, teams

Ordinary Doubles uses an even roster with alternating partners. Direct Team Match lets two teams arrange singles and doubles games without first creating a league.

Every dart. Or the whole visit.

Enter individual darts with Single, Double, Treble, Bull and Miss controls, or enter a completed visit total. Answer the finishing-dart and double prompts when asked so the scorer can apply the rules and record the right detail.

The Match Desk

Large remaining scores, the active player, current visit and match progress keep the next action clear. Checkout suggestions and voice announcements support play.

A match you can return to

Save supported local matches to Saved Games and resume them later. Undo Dart and Undo Turn correct eligible input. Online and fixture-managed matches have their own controls.

TAKE A CHANCE. PLAY POTS.

Add a different tactical edge with Chance Darts Pots. A 180 adds 50 Pot points, 140 adds 40, 120 adds 30 and 100 adds 20.

The power of 26

Any visit totalling 26 takes 10 Pot points from each opposing-side player. It need not be 1, 5 and 20. Teammates are protected, and Pots can go below zero.

The Pot decides

Configured legs or sets determine when play ends. The highest individual Pot wins Singles; partners’ Pots combine in Doubles. Ties use the recorded last-leg winner where eligible, then recorded order.

The Chance Darts difference

A LITTLE CHANCE. A DIFFERENT GAME.

Choose optional Pots when you want more at stake in every visit.
