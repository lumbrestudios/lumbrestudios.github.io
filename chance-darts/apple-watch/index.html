Native Apple Watch companion

YOUR MATCH.
ON YOUR WRIST.

A glance at the game, without reaching for your phone. Follow live scores and match progress with the native companion included with Chance Darts for iPhone.

Download on theApp Store

Included with iPhone app · watchOS 10 or later · Paired iPhone required.

LIVE SCORES.ONE GLANCE.ALL CHANCE.CHANCE DARTS / LUMBRE STUDIOS

Stay with the throw.

View both players’ scores and follow the current player as turns change. The Watch automatically scrolls to the active player’s scorecard, keeping your next glance useful.

Match information

Follow scores, leg and set progress for supported two-player local singles matches. Scoring stays on your iPhone.

The result stays with you

View the last completed match result on the Watch. The iPhone remains the source of the recorded match.

Ready when you return.

Open Chance Darts on your paired iPhone and start a supported local singles match. Open the companion on your Apple Watch to follow it.

Reconnect on reopening

The Watch reconnects to the iPhone when reopened. Check its connection state; retained scores can be historical while the companion is reconnecting.

A focused companion

The accepted Watch scope is two-player local singles. Online matches, training, Pots, doubles and fixture-managed competitions are outside this viewing scope.
