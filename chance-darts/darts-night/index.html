For pubs, clubs & friends

LESS ORGANISING.
MORE DARTS.

Turn a gathering at the board into a proper competition. Fixtures, running order and live standings keep the evening moving.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. Optional lifetime Pro.

SET UP THE NIGHT.

  1. Bring your players

    Choose 3–6 individual players from saved profiles, or add guests for the night. Everyone plays everyone once.

  2. Choose the rules

    Pick 301, 501 or 701, best of 1, 3 or 5 legs, Double In / Double Out and optional Chance Darts Pots. The usual local-play access check applies.

  3. Follow the running order

    Play or resume the available fixture, return its completed result to the night and follow the live table. Six players produces 15 matches.

The whole night, together.

The dashboard brings fixtures, standings and highlights into one place. See the current game, what comes next and how the competition is shaping up.

Save the evening

Resume a saved night from Darts Night Home. At completion, the final standings and highlights give the group its result.

Same players. Another night.

Rematch starts a new night with the previous participants and settings. Ending or abandoning the active night asks for confirmation and keeps completed history.

Give the room a view.

Send Darts Night to browser Live Display or the native Apple TV receiver. On iPhone Duo, score inside and show the action outside, including offline local display.

Between the matches

Live Display can stay with the event through fixture changes and into another night. Stop Display ends the broadcast when you are done.

Choose the right competition

Darts Night is a local round robin for individual players. Use Leagues for a longer season, team rosters and the supported league formats.
