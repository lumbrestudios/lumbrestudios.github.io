1.4.0

25 November 2026

Your match. Your wrist. Your screens.

  • New native Apple Watch companion with live scores, current-player focus, match progress, completed results and reconnection when reopened.
  • New iPhone Duo outer-screen Live Display for ordinary Match Play and Darts Night, including offline use. Camera access enables simultaneous screen operation.
  • Independent controls for local and remote spectator displays, with simultaneous Duo, browser and Apple TV presentation.
  • Refined scoring button graphics, Darts Night setup spacing, and display continuity between matches and rematches.
1.3.0

Earlier release

The foundation for connected play.

  • Online Play with optional live video, adaptive Duo scoring layouts, the browser Live Display and the Match Desk were already present before 1.4.0.
  • Training, player progress, Darts Night and Local and Shared Leagues continue as part of the Chance Darts experience.
TV 1.0

25 November 2026

A dedicated view for Apple TV.

The native Live Display receiver is a separate free download, with Match Play, Darts Night, remote controls and an offline demo.
