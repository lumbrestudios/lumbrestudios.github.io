iPhone Duo

ONE PHONE.
BOTH SIDES
OF THE GAME.

Fold it. Open it. Set it beside the board. Adaptive scoring meets a local outer-screen display that brings the spectators into your match.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. Optional lifetime Pro.

A layout for your angle.

Folded portrait and landscape keep the scorer usable in a compact space. Unfolded portrait and landscape give the Match Desk more room. Tabletop positioning brings the device naturally beside your board.

Turn without starting over

Adaptive scoring layouts retain the active match as the available space changes. Choose the posture that suits where you play.

Room for the details

Unfolded views give the scores, current visit and input controls space. The layouts respond to available display geometry.

Score inside. Watch outside.

Enable Duo Live Display for ordinary Match Play or Darts Night. Enter scores on the inner screen while the outer screen presents the match to spectators.

Local means local

No internet connection, pairing code or extra device is needed for the local Duo display. Browser and Apple TV displays can run alongside it when connected.

Independent controls

Control local Duo and remote spectator destinations separately. Completed results and Darts Night handovers keep the viewing experience with the event.

Why camera access is needed.

Simultaneous inner and outer display operation requires the system camera capture session. Enable Duo Live Display explicitly and allow camera access. The camera indicator is expected while it is enabled.

No camera feed on the display

Duo’s display session does not analyse, record or transmit camera frames. It does not use a microphone. Camera access enables the system’s simultaneous display operation.

One camera feature at a time

Stop Duo Live Display before using Online Video or another camera feature. Backgrounding or a camera interruption stops the local display; re-enable it when ready. Brief screen dimming can occur when moving the device or adjusting the hinge.
