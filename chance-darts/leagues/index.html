THE TEAMS.THE FIXTURES.THE SEASON.CHANCE DARTS / LUMBRE STUDIOS

On this device.

Create a Local League, add teams and players, choose the format, then review and create. Local competition supports round-robin tables and knockout brackets; advanced organiser formats require Pro.

Teams and fixtures

Choose singles and doubles games in the line-up. A player can play once in singles and once in doubles. Recorded results lock the line-up.

Results that count

Play each game, review and save the fixture result. Standings follow the saved points and tie-break settings. Local round-robin leagues can allow draws; winner-required and knockout fixtures need a settled winner.

Shared with your competition.

Sign in to Shared Leagues to work with organisers, captains and teams. Membership and role determine the actions available to each person.

People and schedules

Organisers add teams and invite captains. Captains and organisers manage permitted rosters. Choose one-off or home-and-away pairings, and use the fixture date controls where your role allows.

Submit. Confirm. Resolve.

Submitting a result is separate from confirming it. The opposing captain can confirm or dispute where permitted; organiser override is a distinct action. Pending or disputed results are not confirmed standings results.

Start small. Grow the competition.

One free League Organiser starter is shared across Local and Shared Leagues, for up to four entrants. Creating more leagues, larger competitions and advanced organiser formats requires Pro.

Free to take part

Joining a Shared League and playing its authorised fixtures remain free. Account membership and fixture permissions still apply.

Keep your season intact

Normal edits preserve fixtures. Replacement is separate and restricted once results or game setup have been saved. Deleting a starter league does not reset its allowance.
