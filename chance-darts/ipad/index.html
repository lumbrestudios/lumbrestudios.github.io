Chance Darts on iPad

MORE ROOM.
SAME GAME.

Give the Match Desk space to breathe. Responsive iPad layouts bring larger scores and comfortable scoring controls to your board-side setup.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. Optional lifetime Pro.

Put the match in view.

Use the larger display for scoring and match information, with layouts that respond to the space available. Position the iPad where the scorer can reach it and players can read the game.

Play your format

301, 501, 701, supported singles and doubles, teams and optional Pots use the same Chance Darts rules.

Practice with purpose

All five training games give solo practice and group sessions their own structure. Enter the darts you actually throw and review the session result.

Make it the centre of the night.

Run Darts Night fixtures, follow standings or manage a Local or Shared League from the larger screen. Send a supported match to browser Live Display or the native Apple TV receiver.

Play a friend remotely

Online Play includes real-time scoring and optional camera sharing. Hosting requires Pro; joining is free. Connected features require internet access.

The same optional Pro

The iPhone and iPad app uses the existing lifetime Pro purchase. Restore using the same Apple store account that owns it; app-account sign-in is separate.
